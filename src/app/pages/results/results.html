Before vs After

Real operational change

Before

  • Inventory losses, stock inconsistencies, and limited visibility into product availability.
  • Invoices, contracts, and supporting documents scattered across paper records and isolated files.
  • Stations without reliable consumption data, making operational decisions difficult.
  • Unplanned routes, inefficient scheduling, and poor daily coordination.
  • Estimated income disconnected from actual production and financial performance.

After

  • Real-time inventory visibility with product traceability, last purchase cost tracking, and faster stock lookup.
  • Digital document management that standardizes contracts and invoices into a single, controlled format.
  • Connected stations reporting live consumption metrics and delivering actionable operational dashboards.
  • Optimized route planning and improved execution efficiency across daily field operations.
  • Production and projected income aligned with actual business outcomes for better financial control.

Inventory & stock

Before: inventory losses, fragmented records, and delayed product identification. After: faster product lookup, real-time stock visibility, last-cost tracking, and more reliable purchasing decisions.

Invoice register

Before: contracts and invoices were often lost or stored in inconsistent paper formats. After: documents are captured digitally, standardized, and easier to audit, track, and reconcile.

Electrical consumption

Before: stations lacked consistent information about energy use and operating patterns. After: networked stations report live consumption data through a centralized system that supports informed decision-making.

Landscaping schedule

Before: work orders were spread across the day without clear geographic coordination. After: field teams are grouped more effectively, locations are planned more efficiently, and more tasks are completed within the same operating window.

MeatTrace Manager

Before: production reporting and expected income were not fully aligned, creating uncertainty in operational planning. After: daily production is tracked against estimated financial performance, enabling more accurate forecasting and stronger management control.
